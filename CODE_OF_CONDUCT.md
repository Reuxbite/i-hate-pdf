# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Disagreement about technical choices is welcome; harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, and publication of another person's private data are not.

Examples of welcome behavior include giving reproducible evidence, assuming good intent while discussing impact, accepting correction, and keeping criticism about the work rather than the person.

## Scope

This applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces, as well as when someone publicly represents the project.

## Reporting

Do not publish sensitive evidence. Use the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability** for a private report. Maintainers may remove content, close interactions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

## Attribution

This is a concise project-specific conduct policy informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
